Features

A disciplined record of every trading day.

Purpose-built around the way serious traders plan, execute and review.

01

Structured Daily Journaling

Each trading day is its own page. Entry and exit times are captured automatically as you log, and every timestamp remains fully editable.

02

Automated Risk & P&L Metrics

Enter your levels and the journal computes risk-to-reward, realised P&L and the exact percentage of capital at risk on every trade.

03

Rule Adherence Tracking

Score each trade against your standing rules — Yes, No or Partly — and monitor your discipline rate across days, weeks and months.

04

A Focused, Linked Workspace

A command palette, a vault sidebar, tags and internal links keep your research and past pages a single keystroke away.

05

Performance Analytics

A cumulative equity curve alongside profit factor, expectancy and win rate, with clear breakdowns by symbol and by tag.

06

Private & Local-First

Your journal is stored on your own device. Export or restore your complete history at any time as a single portable file.

Browser extension

One click from your toolbar.

Install the Chrome extension and your journal is always a click away — it opens in a full tab and saves privately inside the browser. Same journal, same data model as the web app.

  1. Download the extension folder and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
  4. Pin it — click the Jr to journal.
