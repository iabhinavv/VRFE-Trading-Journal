Features

Everything a trading journal should be — nothing it shouldn’t.

Built around the way disciplined traders actually review their day.

📅

Daily pages, auto-stamped

Every day is its own page. Entry and exit times stamp themselves the moment you log a trade — and every stamp stays editable.

📈

R/R, P&L & % risked

Enter your levels and the journal computes Risk/Reward, P&L and the exact percentage of your account you put on the line.

✅

“Did I follow my rules?”

Mark every trade Yes / No / Partly against your standing Trader’s Rules, and watch your adherence rate over time.

🧠

Obsidian-style workspace

A vault sidebar, a ⌘K command palette, #tags, [[links]] between days and back-links — think, don’t just log.

📊

Analytics & equity curve

Cumulative P&L curve, profit factor, expectancy, win rate, and breakdowns by symbol and by tag.

💾

Local-first & private

Everything lives on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Back up or restore with a single JSON file.

Workflow

From the opening bell to your post-market review.

1

Log the trade

Hit N, type the symbol and your levels. Times stamp automatically.

2

Write the reason

Capture the setup and context in markdown, with #tags and [[links]] to symbols and days.

3

Score your discipline

Did you follow your rules? Mark it honestly — that’s where the edge compounds.

4

Review & export

Study the week, the month and the equity curve. Export to Obsidian, Excel or Notion.

Browser extension

One click from your toolbar.

Install the Chrome extension and your journal is always a click away — it opens in a full tab and saves privately inside the browser. Same journal, same data model as the web app.

  1. Download the extension folder and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
  4. Pin it — click the V to journal.
